FAQ

Questions, answered.

When do you come out after a storm?

Seasonal clients are dispatched automatically once snowfall reaches the trigger depth (typically 5 cm). Most properties are cleared within a few hours of the snow stopping.

Do you clear the windrow from the city plow?

Yes — the snowbank left at the end of your driveway by city plows is included on every visit.

Is salt safe for pets and new concrete?

We offer pet-friendly and concrete-safe de-icers on request at a small extra cost.

Are you insured?

Ask us for our certificate of insurance — we're happy to provide documentation for property managers and condo boards.

How do I pay?

Seasonal contracts can be paid in full or in instalments. Per-visit service is billed after each clear. E-transfer and major cards accepted.
